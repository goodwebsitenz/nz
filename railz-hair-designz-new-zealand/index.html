FAQ

Common questions

How far ahead should I book?

A week or two usually gets you the time you want; event work is best booked further out.

What if I hate the colour?

Colour is agreed and checked against a strand or swatch before the full application.

Do you do walk-ins?

Where there is a gap, yes — messaging first is the safest way to be sure.

Can I bring a photo?

Please do. A photo or two is the fastest way to agree on a look.
