About

Fitted for your own hair

Extensions only work if your natural hair can carry them, so the first step is checking density and condition. Weight, row placement and colour get agreed before fitting, and removals are done properly rather than pulled out.

FAQ

Common questions

How long does an install take?

Tape and weft installs usually take a few hours; braids can take longer.

Can I colour my extensions?

Some can be toned — it gets checked before purchase.

How often are refits?

Tape and weft refits are usually every six to eight weeks.

Will they damage my hair?

Not when weight and placement suit your hair, which is why the consult comes first.
