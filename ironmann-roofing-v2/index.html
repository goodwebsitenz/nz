Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers to the questions we get most.

How much does a new roof cost?+
Every roof is different — size, pitch, access and material choice all affect the price. We give you a fixed written quote after a free inspection, so there are no surprises.
How long does a reroof take?+
Most residential reroofs take around 5–7 working days, weather depending. You get a clear timeline before we start, and we keep you updated if anything changes.
Do you do both residential and commercial roofing?+
Yes. We run residential and commercial work under one team — from single-home reroofs to multi-unit and warehouse projects. The same standards apply to both.
Are you licensed and insured?+
Yes — we're fully licensed and insured for the work we take on, and every job meets the New Zealand Building Code. Ask us for our details before you commit.
Do you offer emergency roof repairs?+
Yes. We handle urgent leak repairs with fast turnaround where possible — call 021 263 6446 and we'll sort it out.
What roofing materials do you use?+
We install longrun metal, corrugated and trapezoidal profiles, and membrane systems for flat roofs — and we recommend the right system for your building and budget.
Do you offer a warranty?+
Yes. Our workmanship is covered by a warranty, and the materials we install carry manufacturer warranties.
Does a quote cost anything?+
No — quotes are free. We inspect the roof, give you a fixed written quote, and only start work when you're happy.
